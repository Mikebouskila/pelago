# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and technical creators who build with Claude Code and Codex. They open Pelago after weeks or months of work to understand what they have made, rediscover forgotten projects, and turn an invisible local history into something emotionally meaningful and worth sharing.

## Product Purpose

Pelago converts private AI-coding history into a personal, explorable pixel-art archipelago. Each project becomes a recognizable island whose terrain, settlement, and energy encode real aggregate signals. Success means the world is understandable within seconds, rewarding to explore for minutes, and visually distinctive enough that users voluntarily share it.

## Brand Personality

Personal, wondrous, handcrafted. Pelago should feel like opening a beloved strategy-game save file, not inspecting an analytics dashboard. The voice is concise, warm, and quietly proud.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with a stat strip, filter pills, and repeated cards.
- Tiny decorative islands that cannot be distinguished or understood.
- Crypto, neon, metaverse, or gamified-productivity aesthetics.
- Dense developer telemetry, rankings, scores, or claims about code quality.
- Inconsistent one-off fantasy blobs with no stable visual grammar or connection to project data.

## Design Principles

1. The world is the interface. Controls and metrics support it without competing with it.
2. Show the mapping. Every visual difference should be legible and explainable.
3. Reward curiosity. Hover, focus, and selection reveal meaningful details and small surprises.
4. Make sharing intrinsic. Exported cards must look complete, authored, and recognizable without context.
5. Preserve dignity. Activity describes a footprint, never a judgment of productivity or quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for interface text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, touch targets of at least 44px on mobile, and redundant labels for all color-coded agent or category distinctions.
